Rules of the Road

Dos and Don'ts

Do

  • Stick to the €200/day budget. Track it on your phone as you spend — it adds up faster than expected at the bar.
  • Buy beer and water from Mercadona before the beach and before pre-drinks every night. Fraction of bar prices.
  • Split all Ubers four ways every time. Never book solo. This alone saves €15–20 per day across the trip.
  • Pre-book activities. Jet ski, UM Beach House, any restaurant you actually care about. Sunday dinner especially — book it Saturday afternoon.
  • DM Times Square Music Club and Pure Night Club on Instagram before flying. Confirm the afrobeats nights are running on your dates.
  • MC and TT: pre-book the airport Uber on Sunday before going out. Screenshot the confirmation. Do not leave it to the club at 2am.
  • Wear SPF 50 from day one. A burn on Friday means suffering for the remaining three days straight.
  • Drink a litre of water before sleeping each night. You need to be functional for four days back to back.

Don't

  • Take deals from street touts on Punta Ballena. They target tourists. Walk straight past without making eye contact.
  • Take valuables to clubs. Leave watches, good chains, and extra cash in the hotel safe before going out each night.
  • Use non-metered taxis. Uber only — always see the price before getting in.
  • Go too hard at pre-drinks before Pure Night Club. It is a 6am finish. Pace from the hotel forward.
  • Walk back from Magaluf at night. Always take an Uber home. No exceptions, no matter how close it feels.
  • Skip hotel dinner before going out. It is free and included. Using it saves €25+ and lines the stomach for a big night.
  • Get into anything stupid with bars, promoters, or strangers on the strip. Nobody is in Mallorca to deal with drama.
  • Leave Sunday dinner unbooked until Sunday morning. Half the decent restaurants fill up Saturday afternoon.
